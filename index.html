System Overview

Comprehensive tools for immigration and labour oversight.

Real-Time Dashboard

Monitor active permits, expiring visas, and recent registrations at a glance with dynamic charts and KPI cards.

Client Management

Track foreign workers, their country of origin, occupation, and visa history. Manage detailed profile notes.

Establishment Records

Maintain a registry of companies and their sub-companies, ensuring compliance with labour laws.

Permit Issuance

Issue new permits, track expiry dates, and update statuses (Active, Expired, Under Renewal).

System Architecture

How the data flows within the platform.

1. Register Client

Foreign workers are registered and assigned to a specific Establishment.

2. Issue Permit

A Work Permit is generated and linked to the client's profile.

3. Monitor & Audit

Immigration officers track statuses, expiry dates, and compliance.

4. Secure Access

System administrators manage user accounts and CRUD permissions.
